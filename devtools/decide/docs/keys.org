#+TITLE: decide terminal keys

Ctrl+Enter, Alt+Enter, and ~C-s~ send from every composer: the note,
the reply, the answer, and the outcome box. In the note box,
Ctrl+Enter does the same as Tab to Send and then Enter. Enter and
~C-j~ keep their own meaning: ~C-j~ inserts a newline, Enter inserts
one in the note box, and Enter sends in the other boxes. The inbox
runs the terminal in raw mode, which turns off XON/XOFF, so ~C-s~
reaches it.

~Alt+q~ fills the paragraph at the cursor. List items stay separate:
the marker stays on the first line and later lines line up under the
item text. An indented block, and text inside a fence of backticks or
tildes, is left as it is. The width is ~ui.fillColumn~, or 72 when
that is unset, and it is never wider than the compose box.

* Ctrl+Enter

Ctrl+Enter works where the terminal sends it as its own sequence with
no keyboard mode switched on. The two sequences are =ESC [27;5;13~=
(xterm modifyOtherKeys) and =ESC [13;5u= (kitty). The inbox rewrites
those two to Alt+Enter before bubbletea parses the input. Every other
sequence passes through unchanged.

When a read stops partway into one of those sequences, the inbox waits
up to 25ms for the rest, so a lone Esc is at most that late. The inbox
does not turn on modifyOtherKeys or the kitty keyboard protocol.
Either mode would also change how Shift and Ctrl letters, or Esc,
arrive, and bubbletea v1 does not parse those sequences. A terminal
that sends Ctrl+Enter as plain Enter or ~C-j~ gets that key's meaning.

What one terminal sends (herdr, ~TERM=xterm-256color~, no mode
switched on):

| Key        | Arrives as                              |
|------------+-----------------------------------------|
| Ctrl+Enter | =ESC [27;5;13~= (xterm modifyOtherKeys) |
| Alt+Enter  | ~alt+enter~                             |
| ~C-j~      | ~ctrl+j~                                |
| Enter      | ~enter~                                 |

* DECIDE_KEYDEBUG

Set ~DECIDE_KEYDEBUG~ to a log file path, or to ~1~ for
~decide-keys.log~ in the temp directory:

#+begin_src sh
DECIDE_KEYDEBUG=/tmp/decide-keys.log decide inbox
#+end_src

Each key is one line with its bubbletea type, ~alt~, runes, and name,
such as ~key type=13 (enter) alt=true runes="" str="alt+enter"~. A
rewritten Ctrl+Enter adds a line before its ~alt+enter~ key, such as
~ctrl+enter bytes="\x1b[13;5u" as alt+enter~. Other input bubbletea
does not parse is logged with its bytes, such as
~unknown tea.unknownCSISequenceMsg bytes="\x1b[13;2u"~.
