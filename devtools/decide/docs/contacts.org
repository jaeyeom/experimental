#+TITLE: decide contacts and org tree

Contacts (~g~ then ~c~ in ~decide inbox~) lists ~agentbus list~ names
plus every owner and sender. ~human~ and ~system~ are not contacts.

* States and actions

~active~ is alive, ~unresponsive~ is registered but not answering, and
~dead~ is gone or known only from the ledger. ~x~ hides a row until it
is active again. ~H~ shows hidden rows for this session. ~X~ hides every
dead contact after a confirm. ~R~ runs ~agentbus release~ for a name the
bus lists as dead. ~a~ stores a ~contactAliases~ entry. ~*~ stars a
contact, here or in the compose recipient list: starred names come first
in that list, marked ~★~, in the order they were starred, and are not
repeated below. A starred name that is not live still shows there,
marked dead. Hidden and starred do not conflict: the name stays hidden
in Contacts and is still listed first when composing. ~decide contacts
prune --dead~ prints names and writes nothing unless ~--yes~.
~--release~ also releases those bus names.

* Detail panel

The list starts with the name. A panel shows the contact under the
cursor in full: its avatar as the thread shows it, its state, its
liveness and when agentbus last saw it, its whole ~when~ text, its
open, answered, done, and withdrawn item counts, its last activity, and
how many messages to it are still waiting or failed to deliver. On a
wide screen the panel is to the right of the list. Below 80 columns it
is hidden; ~i~ shows it below the list and hides it again, and the
footer names the key.

                                                               1 withdrawn

#+end_example

* Org tree view

With ~ui.orgTree~ on, ~t~ switches Contacts between the list and the
org tree from ~orgctl tree --json~ on ~ui.orgSpec~: you, then direct
reports, then their reports, with each pool under the role that starts
it. Roles with no contact and pools are dimmed and cannot be selected.
Contacts the spec does not name are in an ~other~ group at the bottom.
If ~orgctl~ is missing or fails, the list stays flat with one line
saying why. With ~ui.orgTree~ off, decide never runs ~orgctl~. On a hub
client, ~orgctl~ and ~ui.orgSpec~ stay on the hub: the hub's ~ui.orgTree~
becomes the default (a local ~ui.orgTree: false~ still turns it off),
and the client caches the tree in its local replica, refreshing on
connect and when the hub's spec mtime changes. An older hub without
~org-tree~ falls back to local ~orgctl~ with a one-line note.

{"ui": {"orgTree": true, "orgSpec": "/home/me/org/desk.json"}}
#+end_src
